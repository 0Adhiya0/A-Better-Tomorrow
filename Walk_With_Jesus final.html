👋 New here? How to use this site
  1. In My Journey, log your rosary, short prayers, Bible reading, Holy Mass and good deeds each day, and watch the ring fill.
  2. In Be the Light, choose a real problem, read what Jesus teaches, and do one kind act.
  3. In Pledge a Prayer, promise prayers for people in need and tap “I prayed today”.
  4. In Verses & Gratitude, find comfort for hard times and write a thank-you letter to God.
  5. Everything is saved privately on this device.

Be the Light

“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.” Matthew 5:16 · KJV · Read in NRSV-CE ↗

Choose a real problem in our world. Read what Jesus’ teaching says, reflect, then take one small action. Tap “I did this” to add it to your kindness count.

Pledge a Prayer

Choose a need, name who you are praying for, and promise a prayer. Every time you pray, tap “I prayed today”. Rosary, Mass and Bible pledges also count in your journal.

Prayer and action go together. If someone is in danger or needs help, please connect them with a trusted adult, a counsellor, a doctor or local emergency services.

Pledges are saved only in this browser. Please avoid full names to protect privacy.

Verses for Life’s Problems

Facing something heavy? Search a problem, such as lonely, stress, money, lies or anger, and find Bible verses, comfort and one step to take.

Scripture brings comfort and strength, but it does not replace professional help. If you are struggling with anxiety, depression, abuse or addiction, please also talk to a doctor, counsellor or trusted person.

💌 A Thank-You Letter to God

When your prayers and good works for the day are done, take a quiet moment. Breathe slowly, then write what you are thankful for. Gratitude can calm the mind and help you rest.
